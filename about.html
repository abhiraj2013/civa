ABOUT CIVA

A place where public problems can be seen, supported and understood.

CIVA is a public civic participation service created to make it easier for people to share problems, discover issues around them and peacefully support matters that deserve attention.

01

THE SERVICE

What is CIVA?

CIVA provides a simple public space where people can bring attention to genuine public problems and civic issues.

Users can explore posts, support issues they care about, participate through communities and follow useful public information.

The goal is to encourage peaceful, responsible and constructive civic participation.

02

OUR PURPOSE

Why CIVA exists

Make problems visible

Help public issues reach a wider audience instead of remaining unnoticed.

Bring people together

Give people a place to discover shared concerns and participate constructively.

Encourage peaceful action

Promote responsible participation without encouraging harassment, violence or unlawful activity.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

OUR VISION

OUR PRINCIPLES

Responsible participation

01

02

03
